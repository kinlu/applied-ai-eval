# Signing in with single sign-on

Passwords for workspaces that use single sign-on (SSO) are managed by your company's identity provider, not by Quillbox.
Quillbox support cannot reset or view SSO passwords.
If you are locked out, contact your workspace admin, who can reset access in your identity provider.
Personal Quillbox accounts can reset their password from the sign-in page with Forgot password.
