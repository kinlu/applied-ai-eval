# Pro pricing update

From 1 December 2026, Quillbox Pro costs $10 per month or $96 per year for new subscribers.
Existing Pro subscribers keep their current price ($8 per month or $84 per year) for 12 months after the change, until 1 December 2027.
Subscribers are emailed at least 30 days before their price changes.
Team plan pricing is not changing.
