# Refunds and cancellations

Annual Pro plans can be refunded in full within 30 days of purchase.
After that, you can cancel at any time and keep Pro until the end of the paid year; the remaining months are not refunded.
Monthly plans are not refunded, but you can cancel at any time and keep Pro features until the end of the billing month.
Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method within 5 to 10 business days.
To cancel a Quillbox plan, open Settings > Billing and choose Cancel plan.
